Basic Requirements

1. M.Th (Philosophy and Religion)

The Master of Theology degree program aims to equip the laity for their vocations, as well as pastors, church workers and other ministers for all types of Christian service. Its ATA accreditation process is going on. The programmes cater to those who face challenges of religious and cultural pluralism; those who need to develop a positive biblical response to varying situations in the constantly changing times; those who seek to promote religious and cultural harmony and peace; and those who wrestle with contemporary challenges.

Eligibility & Duration

Men and women who have passed B.D/ M.Div with second class or its equivalent degree from recognized institutions. It is a two-year degree course.

2. STM (Master of Sacred Theology)

Master of Sacred Theology Degree is offered by Vision Theological Seminary. It aims to equip learners with adequate skills in critical and methodological reflection for creative participation in diversified ministries, such as pastoral, teaching, evangelism, mission work etc.

Eligibility & Duration

Men and women who have passed M.Div/M.A (theology) with second class or its equivalent degree from recognized institutions. It is a two-year course.

3. MASTER OF DIVINITY (M.Div)

The Master of Divinity degree is an ATA accredited degree, it is carefully worked out to meet the academic standards of post graduate degrees in universities in India and institutions accredited by Asia Theological Association all over Asia. The degree is recognized by higher educational bodies in various parts of the world for admission to research degrees.

This degree aims to equip students with an understanding of the Bible and its teachings, to give a total view of life in its spiritual, cultural, political and environmental dimensions. It has been designed to equip the whole church for its life and witness. Hence, not only pastors and teachers with in the church, but also Christians actively involved in various walks of life will benefit from this study.

Eligibility & Duration

Men and women who have passed B.A., B.Th., B.A (in ministry) and experienced pastors below B.A can also apply for M.Div degree course as per ATA rule (10/1). It is a three year course for B.A degree holder but two year course for the candidates holding accredited B.Th. or B.A (in ministry) degree with second class mark.

4. MASTER OF ARTS (theology)

Master of Arts in theology degree is offered by Vision Theological Seminary. This course is conducted to acquire adequate knowledge in Christian Theology. This program is designed for those who seek academic achievements.

Eligibility & Duration

It is a three year course for those who have passed B.A, B.Th or B.A (in ministry) and B.D degree holders with second class marks can complete this course in one year.

5. BACHELOR OF DIVINITY (B.D)

It is a two-year course for Bachelor (secular) degree holders. Those who have obtained any Bachelor degree in theology or in ministry can complete in two years. It is fully designed and conducted by Vision Theological Seminary.

6. BACHELOR OF THEOLOGY

This ATA accredited degree course lays more emphasis on contextual, Biblical and practical aspects of the Christian life and ministry. Those who occupied in work, some pastors or evangelist can avail this opportunity and earn a recognized theological degree while working or serving in their respective places.

Eligibility & Duration

Men and women who have passed Pre-Degree course or Higher Secondary Examination (+2) or S.S.L.C passed with any two year Diploma certificates and experienced pastors below Higher Secondary Course can also apply for B.Th degree course as per ATA rule (10/1).

7. BACHELOR OF CHRISTIAN STUDIES (B.C.S)

This diploma course offered by Vision Theological Seminary, S.S.L.C passed can apply for B.C.S Course. [Experienced and those who are in full time ministry will be enrolled to B.C.S without basic qualification as per quota 10/1)].
