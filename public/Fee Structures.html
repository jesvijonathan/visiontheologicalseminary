GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS

Attendance at all the contact classes required over the period of study.

Passing in all the required papers, successfully completing all the assignments, research papers and integrated papers up to the course.

Successful completion of the Thesis and successful performance in Viva-voce.

Payment of all fees at the due time.

Demonstration of familiarity of language, where necessary for the thesis.

Self-improvement in all areas of life such as Christian prudence and spiritual maturity.
