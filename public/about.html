About Us

HISTORY:

Vision Theological Seminary is born out of the deep conviction that action speaks louder than words and is believed that evangelical ministries in India are possible only if committed young men and women are trained in the Bible discipline and adequate theological education; moreover, its goal is deeply rooted in equipping the dedicated men and women for the fulfillment of the great commission of our Lord Jesus Christ. We train persons of any age without denominational discrepancy. Vision Theological Seminary is founded to fulfill the plan of God on 1st August 2001 and it was accredited by ATA in 2015.

Dr.G..David Raj, the founder, principal and co-faculty of Vision Theological Seminary is having profound commitment to give significant theological training to the learners and main emphasis is given to encourage its students in their effective pastoral ministry, evangelistic career and excellence in theological education is the hall mark of Vision Theological Seminary. Apart from ATA degrees, Vision Theological Seminary offers non-ATA theological degrees as well. Vision Theological Seminary has been awarded the “Best Theological Seminary” award by Tamilan TV, Lions Club and Visvaseva Tele-media on 6th September 2009.

LOCATION:

Vision Theological Seminary is situated in the Southwest side of the Parvathipuram Bus Stand in the walkable distance of 600 ft at Ayswarya Garden in the bosom of Mother Nature with the outer set up of mountains and fields, the weather is cool and breezy with clouds drifting over the mountains, giving natural and mild wind.
